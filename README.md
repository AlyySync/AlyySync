## 👨‍💻 What I do

I build AI agents and automation systems that help businesses spend less time on repetitive work. I connect APIs, databases, and language models into workflows that can act on real data and keep people involved when their judgment matters.

My toolkit combines **n8n, Python, Go, and JavaScript**. I work across workflow design, backend integrations, data processing, and deployment, with a focus on clear logic and reliable operation.

## ⚡ My focus

- **AI agents & RAG** — Multi-agent orchestration, knowledge retrieval, intent classification, and context-aware responses.
- **Business automation** — Connecting CRM, customer support, scheduling, invoicing, and communication tools.
- **API integrations** — Making services work together through REST APIs, webhooks, and structured data.
- **Data pipelines** — Web scraping, AI-assisted extraction, ETL, validation, and deduplication.
- **Content automation** — Connecting text, image, voice, and video workflows with publishing tools.
- **Reliability** — Retry logic, monitoring, alerts, approval steps, and human-in-the-loop escalation.

## 🧠 How I build

**Understand the process → Design the workflow → Connect the systems → Handle failures → Monitor and improve**

I care about the details that keep automation useful after it goes live: clean data, predictable behavior, useful error messages, and documentation someone else can follow. When AI is part of a workflow, I use retrieval, validation, and human review to keep its output grounded.
